# 2026 WW Earth Flyby

An interactive animation of near-Earth asteroid **2026 WW** passing inside the Moon's orbit on 18 November 2026.

**Live page:** https://spalkowetz.github.io/2026-ww-flyby/

## The pass

| Time (UTC) | Event |
|---|---|
| 18 Nov, 04:20 | Crosses inside the Moon's distance (384,400 km) |
| **18 Nov, 07:32** | **Closest to Earth: ~231,400 km from Earth's center (0.60 lunar distances), 26.7 km/s** |
| 18 Nov, 08:43 | Closest to the Moon: ~147,900 km |
| 18 Nov, 10:44 | Back outside the Moon's distance |
| 19 Nov, 05:31 | Discovered by the Catalina Sky Survey (code 703) |

The asteroid came in from the direction of the Sun and was found about 22 hours after closest approach. Its absolute magnitude (H ≈ 27.0) suggests a size of roughly 11–24 m.

## Data

- Orbit announcement: [MPEC 2026-W49](https://minorplanetcenter.net/mpec/K26/K26W49.html), IAU Minor Planet Center
- Trajectory: NASA/JPL Horizons, solution JPL#2 (Earth, Moon and planetary perturbations included)
- `trajectory-data.json`: positions in the J2000 ecliptic frame. Geocentric positions of the asteroid and Moon (km) every 10 minutes from 16 to 20 Nov 2026, and heliocentric positions of the asteroid and Earth (AU) every 2 days for 2026–27.

The orbit is based on about one day of observations, so the long-term orbit is still uncertain. The flyby itself is well determined (timing ±1.5 min, 1σ).
